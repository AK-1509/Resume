# Hindi (`hi`)

## The voice to aim for

Spoken, educated, urban Hindi: how a product person in Delhi, Mumbai or
Bengaluru would describe their work to a colleague over coffee. It is Hindi, not
English in Devanagari, but it uses the English words such a person actually
uses for technical and business ideas. The page's readers are recruiters and
founders, so it is friendly and clear, never chatty or slangy.

Address the reader as आप throughout, with the polite imperative (देखें, करें,
जाएँ, not देखो or देखिए mixed in).

## Words

The test is not "is there a Hindi word for this" but "would the speaker above
use it". DeepL often reaches for a formal or Sanskritised coinage where people
use the loanword, and sometimes keeps English where an everyday Hindi word is
the natural choice.

Keep the loanword, in Devanagari, when it is what people say:
प्रोडक्ट, प्रोजेक्ट, स्टार्टअप, प्रोटोटाइप, मॉडल, प्लेटफ़ॉर्म, डेटा, ऐप, डिज़ाइन,
ब्रांड, मार्केटिंग, बिज़नेस, फ़ाइनेंशियल, स्ट्रैटेजी (रणनीति is also common and
fine in a heading), टीम, कैंपेन, इंस्टाग्राम पोस्ट, वैल्यूएशन, ब्लॉकचेन, टेलीहेल्थ.

Prefer the everyday Hindi word when it exists and is common:
देखें (not अवलोकन करें), संपर्क करें or बात करें (not संपर्क स्थापित करें),
ऊपर जाएँ (for "Back to the top"), भाषाएँ, केस स्टडी (people say this),
स्वास्थ्य (health, in running text), दवा (drug, in running text).

Tells of a stiff, bookish translation, with what people say instead:

| Stiff | Natural | Note |
|---|---|---|
| उत्पाद रणनीति | प्रोडक्ट स्ट्रैटेजी | "Product strategy" as a tag |
| वित्तीय प्रतिरूपण | फ़ाइनेंशियल मॉडलिंग | प्रतिरूपण is a dictionary word, not a spoken one |
| अनुप्रयोग | ऐप | for "app" |
| के माध्यम से (everywhere) | से, के ज़रिए | fine once, heavy when repeated |
| किया जाता है / की जाती है | active verb | English passive carried over |
| हेतु | के लिए | officialese |
| उपरोक्त, निम्नलिखित | (rephrase) | government-letter Hindi |

Proper names stay in Latin script exactly as written: Tunity, ChiMED, GoodRx,
Trader Joe’s, MagSafe, aerieREAL, OCS-01, USC, and so on. Do not transliterate
them, and do not inflect them with a suffix glued on (write Tunity का, not
Tunityका).

## Sentences

- **Word order.** Hindi is verb-final. DeepL often keeps an English clause
  order that is grammatical but reads translated. Move the main verb to the end
  and fold "that/which" clauses into a participle or a second sentence: "a
  platform that pays artists every time AI uses their music" reads better as
  ऐसा प्लेटफ़ॉर्म जो… or as two short clauses than as a literal relative chain.
- **Colons and fragments.** English summaries here often start "Meals tuned to
  your health data: an adaptive nutrition platform…". Keep the colon if the
  Hindi reads as a headline plus explanation; otherwise make one sentence.
- **Full stops.** Use the पूर्ण विराम (।) at the end of Hindi sentences, or the
  Latin full stop, but one of them for the whole page. Where the English has no
  full stop (tags, labels), the Hindi has none either.
- **Gender and agreement.** प्रोजेक्ट is masculine (प्रोजेक्ट देखें, यह प्रोजेक्ट),
  केस स्टडी feminine. Check verbs and postpositions agree with the noun DeepL
  chose, especially after you swap a word.
- **Nukta.** Write ज़, फ़ where the loanword has them (डिज़ाइन, फ़ाइनेंशियल,
  प्लेटफ़ॉर्म), and do it the same way every time.

## Labels on this page

UI strings are short and DeepL sees them alone, so they are where it slips most:

- "Dark" and "Light" are the names of the colour modes on the theme switch,
  not adjectives about a thing (डार्क / लाइट are what people say; गहरा / हल्का
  read as describing a colour).
- "Index" is the tab that opens the list of entries: सूची reads naturally.
  अनुक्रमणिका is a book index and too formal.
- "Site" labels the site navigation for screen readers; साइट is fine.
- "Export" and "Export resume" are a button that saves the résumé as a PDF:
  एक्सपोर्ट and रिज़्यूमे एक्सपोर्ट करें, or a plain verb people use (डाउनलोड करें)
  only if it does not change what the button does. It does make a file, so
  डाउनलोड is acceptable; निर्यात is the trade word for exporting goods and wrong
  here.
- "Get in touch" is an invitation: बात करें or संपर्क करें, not a literal
  "touch" phrase.
- Months and date ranges: अगस्त–दिसंबर 2025, with the en dash and Latin digits.
- Team tags ("Team of three") are a count of people: तीन लोगों की टीम.

## When to flag rather than fix

- A term with two accepted forms where the choice is taste (रणनीति vs
  स्ट्रैटेजी in a heading).
- Domain terms in pharma and finance where you are not sure what a Hindi-speaking
  professional in that field says (e.g. "Phase 3 trials", "break-even",
  "base-case").
- Anything about the person's role or credentials (degree names, job titles):
  a wrong word here misstates who they are, so ask rather than improve.
